"You Manage Your Shop, We'll Manage Your Bills"

Platform Overview

The Market Masters is a comprehensive digital POS, billing, inventory, and shop management solution built for modern shopkeepers and retailers. It streamlines daily business operations, automates billing calculations, tracks profits, and empowers customers to order online directly from local stores.

Key Features & Capabilities

Smart Billing & Thermal Receipts

Generate instant digital bills with auto MRP calculations, shopkeeper cost price profit tracking, customer details, and printable receipts.

Inventory & Stock Management

Real-time stock level tracking, low-stock notifications, barcode scanner support, and restock alerts.

Expense & Distributor Tracking

Categorized daily, monthly, and yearly expense tracking with distributor/agency details and auto-inventory restock item linking.

Payments & Customer Dues

Track paid bills, pending dues, partial payments, and payment modes (Cash, UPI, Card, Cheque, Online) with custom payment status badges.
